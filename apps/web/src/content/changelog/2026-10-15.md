# Writing days and invite links that expire

## New

- **Writing days.** Your profile shows a year of the days you wrote, shaded by how long you spent writing, with your current and longest streak. Every story counts, private ones included, and a switch in your settings hides it from everyone but you.
- **Invite links expire.** When you make a new invite link, you choose whether it lasts 1, 7, or 30 days, with 7 picked to start. Each link in the story's settings shows when it runs out, and opening an expired one says to ask whoever sent it for a new one.
- **Twelve writers per story.** That's the host plus eleven, one for each author color. When a story is full, its settings say so, and friends already invited can join once a writer leaves or is removed.

## Improved

- **Confirmations.** Deleting a chapter or entry, removing a writer, restoring a snapshot, and the other questions the browser used to ask now open in Coscribe's own dialog. If the action fails, the dialog stays open and shows why.
- **Menus and popovers.** Escape closes only the one on top. A dialog opened from a menu leaves the menu where it was.
- **Sidebar on smaller screens.** Where the sidebar opens over the page, keyboard focus moves into it and goes back to where you were when it closes. Following a link from it puts focus on the new page's heading.

## Fixed

- **Smart punctuation.** A quote, `--`, or `...` sometimes stayed as typed, usually when you'd just started typing in a new spot or after a break. It converts every time now.
