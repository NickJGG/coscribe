# Lists in chapters, and notes on entries and the story

## New

- **Lists in chapters.** Chapters have bulleted and numbered lists, from the list buttons in the toolbar or Ctrl+Shift+8 and Ctrl+Shift+7. A list can nest and can sit inside a block quote. Typing a dash or "1." doesn't start one in a chapter, the way it does in a story bible entry, and Listen reads out each item's number.
  ![A numbered list at the end of a chapter, with a nested item under the second one.](chapter-lists.webp)
- **Notes on entries.** Select text in a story bible entry and add a note, just as in a chapter. It gets a card in the margin and a place in the notes panel, and replies, mentions, and resolving it send notifications as they do for a chapter. Pinned and outline entry cards show how many open notes an entry has, and the count opens the entry with the notes panel showing.
- **Notes on a whole chapter or entry.** The add note button at the top of the notes panel writes a note on the chapter or entry as a whole instead of a passage. These come first in the open notes, and their cards line up with the title in the margin.
  ![A chapter title with a note on the whole chapter beside it in the margin.](whole-chapter-note.webp)
- **Story notes.** For talk about the story itself, the notes panel has a story tab next to the chapter's or entry's own, each with its count of open notes. The overview lists the five story notes with the latest activity; clicking one opens it in the panel.
  ![The open notes section of a story's overview, with two story notes and a show all button.](story-notes.webp)
- **Link previews.** Links to Coscribe, to a public story, to a profile, and to an invite link show a card when you paste them into a chat. A story's card has its title, blurb, and writers over a band of their colors, and says whether it's finished. An invite link's card says who invited you, to which story, who else writes it, and when the link expires.
  ![The link preview card for The Lighthouse Keepers, being written by demo_june, with its blurb.](story-card.webp)

## Improved

- **Snapshot compare.** Quoting a paragraph, changing its alignment, moving it into or out of a script, and changing a script line's element all count as changes now, as list changes already did. The space a change takes up is tinted, on the newer side for what a paragraph gained and on the older side for what it lost.

## Fixed

- **Shortcuts on a Mac.** Shortcut hints said Ctrl on a Mac, where the key is Command. They show Mac symbols now, like ⌘B and ⇧⌘S.
