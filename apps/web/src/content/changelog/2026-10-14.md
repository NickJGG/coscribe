# Curly quotes, line breaks, and your account

## New

- **Smart punctuation.** Quotes curl as you type or paste, `--` turns into an em dash, and `...` into an ellipsis. It's on in every story, and the host can switch it off in the story's settings.
  ![A typed line with curly quotes, an em dash, and an ellipsis.](smart-punctuation.webp)
- **Line breaks.** Shift+Enter starts a new line inside a paragraph. The toolbar has a button for it too, and the breaks carry through to the reader and to exports.
  ![Three short logbook lines in one paragraph, below an ordinary paragraph.](line-breaks.webp)
- **Delete your account.** It's at the bottom of your settings, and it asks you to hand each story you host to another writer first. What you wrote in other writers' stories stays, credited to "A deleted account".
- **Download your data.** A button in your settings downloads a .zip of your profile, friends, notes, and story chat messages, plus a Markdown copy of every story you write in.
- **Back to top.** Once you're a screen down in a chapter, the story bible, or the reader, a round button at the bottom takes you back up.
  ![The end of a chapter, with the round back-to-top button below it.](back-to-top.webp)
- **Changelog.** You're reading it. It lives in your account menu and the footer, and a dot on your avatar means there's an update you haven't read.

## Improved

- **Settings.** Your settings and story settings are laid out in cards, and the bio box has room for two lines.

## Fixed

- **Importing outlines.** Importing a file with chapter outlines no longer fails.
